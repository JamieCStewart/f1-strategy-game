# ADR-0008: Player-facing ensemble predictions of future lap times

The player's UI should show ensemble predictions of future lap times, and derived quantities like gap evolution for each candidate strategy.

A **prediction service** runs inside the race-room worker, reusing the batched rollout engine (ADR-0007) and the same posterior draws that drive the live race:

- On entering `decision_pending` (and on player request between decisions), the worker runs N rollouts (default 1,000, configurable per difficulty/budget) for each candidate strategy from the *current* race state.
- Summarization happens **server-side**. The `DecisionPrompt` payload carries, per candidate strategy: per-future-lap lap-time quantiles (p10/p25/p50/p75/p90), derived gap-to-rival quantiles for designated rivals, pit-loss distribution, and a thin sample of ~20 individual traces for "spaghetti" rendering. Raw rollout matrices and posterior parameters never leave the server.
- The frontend renders these as fan charts (shaded quantile bands + faint sample traces) in the strategy view and decision modal — the visual language of broadcast strategy graphics.
- **Honesty rule:** the ensemble shown to the player is generated by the same model that generates reality, at the same fidelity the default-difficulty AI uses. The player's edge is judgment, not information asymmetry. Difficulty modifiers may later widen this (reduced rollout budgets, hidden quantiles, delayed updates) — recorded then as a separate ADR.
- Compute budget: prediction rollouts must complete within a bounded latency target (initially ≤1 s per decision prompt), enforced by the ADR-0007 benchmark suite; rollout count degrades gracefully under load rather than blocking the room.

## Consequences

- The feature is nearly free: it is the AI strategists' planning machinery with a summarization layer and a chart.
- Payloads stay small (quantiles, not 1,000 traces), keeping WebSocket fan-out cheap.
- Model calibration (§5.3) becomes *player-facing*: a poorly calibrated model now misleads users visibly, which raises the stakes of — and the justification for — the CI calibration gate.
- A strong portfolio artifact: "the UI shows live posterior-predictive fan charts per strategy" is precisely what real strategy tools do.
